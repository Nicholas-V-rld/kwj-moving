Your booking system, step by step

Everything a customer sends you lands in three places: your email, your Google Drive and one sheet. This page shows where to look and what to do next.

Video walkthroughs

The videos use example customers. Real bookings work the same way.

1. A customer books

  1. The customer taps Book online on kwjmoving.com, or you text them the link: kwjmoving.com/book/
  2. They enter their details, upload the route video and up to 15 photos, read your terms and sign with their finger.
  3. They see "Thank you, we have it." Nothing else is needed from them yet.

2. You get an email

Within a minute your KWJ inbox gets "New booking request: name (moving date)". It lists the customer's details and has one link: Open the route video, photos and signed booking in Google Drive. Tap it.

3. Everything is in your Drive

Your kwjbookings folder gets one new folder per booking, named by date and a short code.

Inside each booking folder you will find the route video, the photos, the signature and the signed booking PDF.

4. All bookings in one list

Open the KWJ Bookings sheet in the same folder. Every booking adds one row: when it came in, name, phone, email, from, to, moving date, number of files and a link to its folder. Sort or filter it like any spreadsheet. Please do not rename the sheet or the kwjbookings folder; the system finds them by name.

5. Send your flat rate

  1. Watch the video and look at the photos.
  2. Text or call the customer with your binding flat rate.
  3. Payment is Milestone 3: once Clover is connected, the customer pays the full flat rate through your Clover payment link and the sale flows into QuickBooks.

6. Good to know

  1. Test items: the folder 2026-11-01-ntovjd and the TEST rows are from my tests. Delete them any time.
  2. Big videos: up to 500 MB per video and 25 MB per photo. If a customer's video is too big, ask them to text it to you instead.
  3. Changing your terms: send me the new wording and I update the form. Every signed PDF keeps the version the customer agreed to.
  4. Something looks wrong: message me on Upwork with the customer's name and I will check it the same day.
