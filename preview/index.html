What to put in your video

The more we can see, the more exact your price. Two minutes of video is usually enough.

  • Every room

    Walk room to room and open closets. Include the basement, attic or garage if anything is stored there.

  • The way out

    Show the stairs, elevators and hallways, and where a truck can park at both homes.

  • Big and fragile items

    Take photos of couches, beds, dressers and appliances, plus anything heavy like a piano or safe.

Questions before you book

How do I get a price?

Text 848.204.0790 with a walk-through video of your home, photos of large items, and both addresses. We reply with one binding flat rate for the whole move.

What does binding flat rate mean?

The price we give you is the price you pay. There's no hourly clock and no moving-day add-ons for the items and route you showed us.

Why do I pay the full amount when I sign?

Paying the full flat rate at signing locks in your date and your price. We don't take small deposits or partial payments, so there's nothing left to settle on moving day.

How do I pay?

By secure card payment, right after you sign your contract.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. KWJ Moving LLC is State Licensed & Fully Insured in New Jersey, License No.:39PM00506700.

What if I add items after I get my price?

Send a video or photos of anything new before moving day and we'll confirm your flat rate. Your price covers what you showed us.
